## 📚 Courses

- 🆕 **[SCST1133 Data Engineering Ecosystem](https://drshahizan.github.io/scst1133/)**: Introduces students to computing systems, digital tools, data engineering concepts, industry practices and the data engineering lifecycle. Students learn to design, document and communicate data-driven solutions while applying systems thinking, ethical principles and professional responsibilities. Learning materials and activities are available on the **[course website](https://drshahizan.github.io/scst1133/)**, while exercises are submitted through the **[SCST1133 GitHub repository](https://github.com/drshahizan/scst1133)**.

- 🆕 **[High Performance Data Processing](https://github.com/drshahizan/HPDP)**: Covers techniques and tools for processing data at high speeds for performance-critical applications.

- **[Database](https://github.com/drshahizan/database)**: Introduces students to the fundamentals of database systems and their applications in everyday and professional contexts. The course focuses on key concepts and techniques in database development, particularly conceptual and logical design. Topics include database environments, design principles, entity-relationship diagrams, normalization and Structured Query Language (SQL). Students learn to use a Database Management System (DBMS) to manage and manipulate data efficiently.
